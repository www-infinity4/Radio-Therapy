Theory-Backed Facts

  • Electrical power in DC circuits is the product of voltage and current: P = V × I.
  • Electromagnetic wavelength is inversely proportional to frequency: λ = c / f.
  • Lower frequency signals generally have longer wavelengths and require larger antennas for resonance.
